PegShield

On-chain collateral circuit breaker for Solana LST lending.

One-Line Pitch

PegShield is a Solana-native collateral circuit breaker that turns static LST collateral factors into live on-chain borrow limits. Price oracles tell lenders what collateral is worth; PegShield tells lenders whether to keep extending credit against it right now, and at what LTV.

Problem

Solana lending protocols use price oracles, but collateral factors are still static tables. That is dangerous for liquid staking tokens: an LST can keep returning a valid market price while its reference-rate peg, liquidity, and liquidation safety deteriorate faster than governance can react.

Solution

PegShield publishes a compact RiskState PDA per LST. The PDA stores suggested_ltv_bps, regime_flag, freshness, and fixed-point model diagnostics. A lending protocol reads it during borrow evaluation and clamps new credit when the oracle detects peg stress or stale state.

Judge Proof

make frontier-proof

This command uses committed artifacts to show the core behavior: the same borrow passes a static 80% LTV table and is rejected under PegShield stress LTV. It also summarizes the June 2022 stETH replay where PegShield cut LTV before the fixed baseline showed modeled shortfall. 

make multi-attester-demo

This command shows the threshold path: registry initialization, three bonded attesters, 2-of-3 proposal confirmation, finalized RiskState, and readiness checks. 

make scenario-lab

This command summarizes all historical and synthetic stress scenarios, including first LTV cut, first critical signal, modeled loss prevention, and false-positive recovery.

What Works Today

  • Anchor risk-oracle program deployed on Solana devnet.
  • RiskState PDA storing live LTV, regime, timestamp, and fixed-point risk parameters.
  • Pyth plus protocol reference-rate bridge for mSOL-v2, jitoSOL-v1, and bSOL-v1.
  • OU/ADF risk engine that converts peg deviation into suggested LTV.
  • Source-quality haircuts for fallback history, fallback reference rates, missing liquidity depth, and wide Pyth confidence.
  • Typed SDK, operator CLI, updater scripts, PegShield Gate, and offline borrow-gate demo.
  • Deterministic multi-attester proof for the 2-of-3 finalization path.
  • Historical stETH replay plus synthetic stress scenarios.

Why Solana

The value is enforcement inside the borrow path. Solana makes it cheap for protocols to read a PDA, enforce a fresh LTV, and avoid waiting for off-chain reports or governance updates during a fast LST stress event.

Honest Status

PegShield is hackathon-stage devnet infrastructure. It is not audited, not mainnet production infrastructure, and independent attester operations are not live yet. The submission proves the primitive, the integration path, and the stress behavior.
